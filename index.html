10 · Privacy

Private by design.

🔐

Access control

User-controlled account access and session security.

🗃️

Document vault

Keep uploaded medical documents organized rather than scattered.

🧾

Auditability

Show when information was added, edited or imported.

Important product principle:

This prototype is for organization and education. A real deployment would require strong security, appropriate consent, data-protection controls and careful medical-safety review.
